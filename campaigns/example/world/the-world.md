---
title: The World
kind: lore
---

The grey port of **Saltmarrow** clings to the edge of a cold northern sea, its
lanes slick with rain and fish-oil. Since the [[Ash War]] ended a generation ago,
the town has been quiet — too quiet, the old dockhands say. Ships still come, but
fewer, and the lighthouse on the far mole has been dark for a year.

You arrive as a stranger with a name no one here knows.
